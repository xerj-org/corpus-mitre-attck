T1435 | Access Calendar Entries | domain: mobile-attack | matrix: mitre-mobile-attack | status: revoked; superseded by T1636.001 (Calendar Entries)

DESCRIPTION
An adversary could call standard operating system APIs from a malicious application to gather calendar entry data, or with escalated privileges could directly access files containing calendar data.

KILL CHAIN PHASES
collection (mitre-mobile-attack)

PLATFORMS
