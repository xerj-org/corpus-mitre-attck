T1079 | Multilayer Encryption | domain: enterprise-attack | matrix: mitre-attack | status: revoked; superseded by T1573 (Encrypted Channel)

DESCRIPTION
An adversary performs C2 communications using multiple layers of encryption, typically (but not exclusively) tunneling a custom encryption scheme within a protocol encryption scheme such as HTTPS or SMTPS.

KILL CHAIN PHASES
command-and-control (mitre-attack)

PLATFORMS
Linux
macOS
Windows
