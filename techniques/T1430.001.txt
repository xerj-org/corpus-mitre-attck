T1430.001 | Remote Device Management Services | domain: mobile-attack | matrix: mitre-mobile-attack

DESCRIPTION
An adversary may use access to cloud services (e.g. Google's Android Device Manager or Apple iCloud's Find my iPhone) or to an enterprise mobility management (EMM)/mobile device management (MDM) server console to track the location of mobile devices managed by the service.(Citation: Krebs-Location)

KILL CHAIN PHASES
collection (mitre-mobile-attack)
discovery (mitre-mobile-attack)

PLATFORMS
Android
iOS

MITIGATIONS
- Enterprise Policy
- User Guidance

DETECTION STRATEGIES
- DET0702 Detection of Remote Device Management Services
