T1436 | Commonly Used Port | domain: mobile-attack | matrix: mitre-mobile-attack | status: deprecated

DESCRIPTION
Adversaries may communicate over a commonly used port to bypass firewalls or network detection systems and to blend with normal network activity to avoid more detailed inspection. 

They may use commonly open ports such as

* TCP:80 (HTTP)
* TCP:443 (HTTPS)
* TCP:25 (SMTP)
* TCP/UDP:53 (DNS)

They may use the protocol associated with the port or a completely different protocol.

KILL CHAIN PHASES
command-and-control (mitre-mobile-attack)
exfiltration (mitre-mobile-attack)

PLATFORMS
Android
iOS
